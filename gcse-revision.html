GCSE revision flashcards built around your specification

Choose your exam board and subject. Cognito writes practice in that course’s language, tracks it across the topic list, and spaces your reviews up to exam day.

Re-reading feels productive, but the revision habits with the strongest evidence are answering questions from memory and spreading that practice out over weeks (Dunlosky et al., 2013). Cognito is a flashcard app built around both, with your GCSE course as context: choose your board and subject, and your practice is written and tracked against that specification.

What your course changes

Question style
Cards use the course’s terminology and question style. Your material still decides which facts appear.
Tier
Foundation or Higher, so questions aim at the paper you will actually sit.
Topic coverage
Practice is tracked across the course’s topics, so gaps are easy to spot. It is a study guide, not the complete official specification or a predicted grade.
Command words
In AQA Biology (8461), Chemistry (8462) and Physics (8463), questions use the specification’s command words, such as state, describe, explain, compare and evaluate.
Marks
A tariff such as [3 marks] appears only when your source gives one. Cognito does not invent mark schemes.
Exam papers
For AQA’s separate sciences, the course view lists each paper’s length and marks, as published by AQA.

Turn your notes into cards you trust

Start from a topic, pasted class notes or a photo of a textbook page. Cognito drafts the cards, and you check and edit each one in a preview before it is saved. An inaccurate card turns into a repeated mistake, so that check matters.

The free plan includes 10 AI generation batches a month on each device, plus one free try of a PDF, scan, audio, YouTube or web source. Premium removes the monthly limit and keeps every source available. Hand-made cards are unlimited on every plan, and Anki decks can be imported for free.

Space it out to exam day

Each review ends with a rating — Again, Hard, Good or Easy — and the FSRS scheduler uses it to choose when the card returns: tomorrow if it slipped, a couple of weeks later if it was easy. Try a review on the home page.

Add your exam date to get an Exam Plan. Set the weekdays you study and how many cards fit in a day; Cognito shows each day’s reviews and new cards, carries missed work forward, and keeps the final days before the exam for review. How to build a plan that fits your week.

Exam Plans and FSRS review are free. Plans live on iPhone and iPad, and your cards and review history also sync to Cognito for Mac, which is free to download too.
