A-level revision flashcards built around your specification

Choose your exam board and subject. Cognito writes practice in that course’s language, tracks it across the topic list, and spaces your reviews up to exam day.

A-level content is dense and the exams reward precise recall under pressure. The revision habits with the strongest evidence are answering questions from memory and spreading that practice out over weeks (Dunlosky et al., 2013). Cognito is a flashcard app built around both, with your A-level course as context.

What your course changes

Question style
Cards use the course’s terminology and question style. Your material still decides which facts appear.
Topic coverage
Practice is tracked across the course’s topics, so gaps are easy to spot. It is a study guide, not the complete official specification or a predicted grade.
Command words
In AQA Biology (7402), questions use the specification’s command words, such as describe, explain, analyse, evaluate and calculate.
Marks
A tariff such as [6 marks] appears only when your source gives one. Cognito does not invent mark schemes.
Rich content
Cards can hold LaTeX equations, code, tables and diagrams, which matters in Maths, the sciences and Computer Science.

Turn your notes into cards you trust

Start from a topic, pasted lecture or class notes, or a photo of a page. Cognito drafts the cards, and you check and edit each one in a preview before it is saved. An inaccurate card turns into a repeated mistake, so that check matters.

The free plan includes 10 AI generation batches a month on each device, plus one free try of a PDF, scan, audio, YouTube or web source. Premium removes the monthly limit and adds arXiv papers and every other supported source. Hand-made cards are unlimited on every plan, and Anki decks can be imported for free.

Space it out to exam day

Each review ends with a rating — Again, Hard, Good or Easy — and the FSRS scheduler uses it to choose when the card returns: tomorrow if it slipped, a couple of weeks later if it was easy. Try a review on the home page.

Add your exam date to get an Exam Plan. Set the weekdays you study and how many cards fit in a day; Cognito shows each day’s reviews and new cards, carries missed work forward, and keeps the final days before the exam for review. How to build a plan that fits your week.

Exam Plans and FSRS review are free. Plans live on iPhone and iPad, and your cards and review history also sync to Cognito for Mac, which is free to download too.
